About Us

Randhart Tyres started as a two-bay yard on the edge of Alberton and grew because the work was done properly the first time.

Our story

From one bay to four

Randhart Tyres opened its doors as a small fitment yard, taking on the jobs bigger dealerships didn't want to deal with — one tyre, one puncture, one honest opinion at a time.

Word travelled because the alignment held, the price matched the quote, and nobody was sent home with tyres they didn't need. Four bays and a full-time crew later, that's still the whole business model.
